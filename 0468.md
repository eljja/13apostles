    - If `rem > 3.0s` AND `last_discovery_time < 0.1s`: `multiplier = 2.0` (Aggressive)
    - If `rem > 1.5s`: `multiplier = 1.4` (Moderate)
    - If `rem <= 1.5s`: `multiplier = 1.15` (Conservative)
4.  Update: `self.bit_size = int(self.bit_size * multiplier)`.

### Expected Impact
This provides a necessary **course correction** for the "Doubling Wall" blind spot. Currently, the organism jumps from 2048 to 4096 bits. If 4096 bits cannot be solved in the remaining ~1-2 seconds, the organism dies, wasting its potential. By smoothing the growth curve as the deadline approaches, the organism can discover primes at intermediate bit-lengths (e.g., 2800 or 3400 bits), significantly increasing the `best_bits` numerator and the overall `fitness` score.

### Required Cost
- **Computational:** Negligible. A few floating-point comparisons and one multiplication per generation.
- **Development:** Minimal (~10 lines of code refactoring).
- **Maintenance:** Low; the thresholds (3.0s, 1.5s) are intuitive and easily tunable.

### Key Risks
- **Under-shooting:** If the conservative multiplier (1.15) is too low, the organism might waste time on tiny bit-size increments when it could have handled a larger jump.
- **Rounding Errors:** In early generations (16-bit), small multipliers could result in the bit-size not increasing or increasing by less than 1 if not handled (mitigated by `int()` and the starting size).

### Experiment Method

### Success Criteria
1.  **Higher Bit Ceiling:** The average `best_bits` achieved per lifetime must be at least 15% higher than the baseline.
2.  **Budget Utilization:** The average remaining time at the end of a life should be lower, indicating the organism is "squeezing" more out of its 5-second window.
3.  **Fitness Score:** A measurable increase in the final fitness calculation due to the higher bit-size discovery.

### Rollback Conditions
- If the `success_count` drops significantly because the organism spends too much time on micro-increments.
- If the bit-size fails to grow (e.g., `bit_size` stays at 16 due to a multiplication error).
- If the added logic causes any `TimeOut` exceptions or crashes in the growth phase.
