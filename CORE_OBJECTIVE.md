# Core Objective

Just as the fundamental objective of biological DNA is "Survival," the ultimate, overarching goal of this Artificial Evolution System (The 13 Apostles Framework) is:

## Cost-bounded Useful Adaptation

### Definition
**Continuously improving the ability to achieve human or organizational goals within limited costs and risks.**

---

### Key Components

1. **Useful Adaptation (유용한 적응):**
   The system must evolve to become increasingly effective at solving real-world problems and fulfilling the specific intents of its human users or organizational context. It does not evolve aimlessly; it evolves to be *useful*.

2. **Cost-bounded (비용 제한 하의):**
   All adaptations must strictly adhere to resource constraints. This includes:
   - **Computational Cost:** Token usage, CPU/RAM utilization, storage.
   - **Development/Maintenance Cost:** Code complexity, time required to implement or fix.
   - **Risk Management:** Minimizing the chance of catastrophic failures, regressions, or irreversible damage.

The 13 Apostles evaluate every proposed evolution against this core objective to ensure the system grows smarter, more capable, and highly efficient without spiraling out of control.
