# Candidate Proposal Format

All evolution candidates proposed by the Apostles MUST strictly adhere to the following format. The identity of the proposing Apostle is intentionally omitted from the final candidate list to prevent bias during the voting process.

```markdown
## Candidate Name
[A concise, clear title for the proposed evolution]

### Core Change
[A specific, technical description of exactly what will be modified, added, or removed in the system]

### Expected Impact
[The anticipated benefits, focusing on how this aligns with the Core Objective (Cost-bounded Useful Adaptation)]

### Required Cost
[An estimation of computational costs (tokens, memory), development time, and future maintenance overhead]

### Key Risks
[Potential negative side-effects, security vulnerabilities, or chances of catastrophic failure]

### Experiment Method
[How this candidate will be tested in a safe, isolated, or small-scale environment before full integration]

### Success Criteria
[The specific, measurable metrics or outcomes that will determine if the experiment was successful]

### Rollback Conditions
[The exact triggers or failure states that will cause the system to immediately abort the experiment and revert to the previous branch]
```
